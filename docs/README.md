# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome! This README provides an overview and entry point for all project management process documentation used at OctoAcme.

## Summary of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme employs a structured, collaborative approach to project management designed to ensure alignment, transparency, and continuous improvement across cross-functional teams. The process is anchored by clear phases—Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective—with each phase guided by lightweight but actionable documentation. Project initiation emphasizes defining business needs, measurable outcomes, and stakeholder alignment; deliverables include a one-pager, stakeholder list, risk assessment, and resources outline. Planning then breaks work into prioritized backlogs, defines acceptance criteria, manages dependencies, and establishes a "Definition of Done," setting a strong foundation for execution.

The framework clearly distinguishes core personas: Project Managers coordinate delivery, manage risk, and facilitate communication; Product Managers own the product vision, set priorities, and measure outcomes; Developers design and implement features, tests, and documentation; QA/testers validate quality; and stakeholders provide key input and approvals. Responsibilities and goals for each role are explicitly defined to foster collaboration and reduce ambiguity. Critical artifacts—such as the project charter, roadmap, backlog, risk register, and retrospective notes—are maintained to provide a living record of project progress and decisions.

Execution centers around well-defined team rhythms: daily standups for status and blockers, weekly delivery syncs, and regular demos at milestone completion. OctoAcme leverages project boards for real-time tracking with clear status columns (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, Review, QA, Done) and a disciplined pull request workflow including automated testing, linting, and peer reviews. Quality assurance is built in throughout the process—unit, integration, and end-to-end tests are paired with CI security scans, and manual QA is performed as needed. Detailed checklists for execution and release ensure critical steps aren't missed.

Risk management and effective communication are central pillars. Risks are actively captured and tracked in a register, evaluated for likelihood and impact, and reviewed regularly. Escalation paths are clearly documented, ensuring issues are triaged efficiently and stakeholders are kept informed. Communication cadences—such as twice-weekly standups, weekly PM/Product Manager syncs, and monthly stakeholder updates—ensure alignment and visibility. After each milestone or incident, a retrospective is held to drive actionable improvements, which are then tracked and reviewed, fostering a culture of learning and continuous improvement.
